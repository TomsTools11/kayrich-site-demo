There are two products and a lot of noise around them.

Term

A fixed number of years for a fixed premium. Almost always the right answer when there is a mortgage and children at home, because it covers the window where losing the income would be catastrophic.

It is cheap because most people outlive it. That is the product working, not failing.

Whole life

Permanent, with cash value. It costs considerably more per dollar of coverage, and it is the right tool for final expenses, an estate, or a business buy-sell. It is the wrong tool for replacing income on a budget.

What people ask on the first call.

How much do I need?

Enough to clear the mortgage and replace the income for as long as the youngest child is at home. We work it out on the call rather than handing you a calculator.

Do I need a medical exam?

Often not, at smaller face amounts. Where an exam does apply it is a nurse at your kitchen table, not a hospital visit.

Is the policy at work enough?

It is usually one or two times salary and it ends when the job does. Treat it as a bonus on top of a policy you own.

Can I cover my children?

Yes, usually as a small rider. It is not an investment and we will not sell it as one, but it does guarantee they can buy coverage later regardless of health.
