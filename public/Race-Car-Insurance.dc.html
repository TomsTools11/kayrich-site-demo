What gets covered, and where the line sits.

On-track physical damage

Damage to the car during competition and hot laps. This is the cover standard policies exclude outright, and the reason racers end up self-insuring by accident.

Off-track physical damage

Fire in the shop, theft out of the trailer, and the wreck on the way home. Most of the total losses in racing happen with the engine off.

Trailer and motorhome

Stacker, gooseneck or coach, valued as the rig it is rather than as a recreational vehicle that sits in a driveway.

Parts and tools

Spare motors, transmissions, wheels and the toolbox. Scheduled at real replacement cost, not a blanket number someone guessed at.
